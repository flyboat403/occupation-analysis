Description
Cyber incident responders monitor and assess cybersecurity state systems, analysing,
evaluating, and mitigating the impact of cybersecurity incidents. Moreover, they identify
malicious actors and cyber incidents root causes. According to the organisation’s Incident
Response Plan, they restore systems and process functionalities to an operational state,
collecting evidence and documenting actions taken.
