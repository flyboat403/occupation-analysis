Description
Golf instructors train and teach golf to individuals or groups. They train their clients by
demonstrating and explaining techniques such as the correct posture and swinging
techniques. They give feedback on how a student can do exercises better and improve the
skill level. The golf instructor advises what equipment is best suited for the student.
