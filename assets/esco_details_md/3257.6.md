Description
Transport health and safety inspectors are responsible for maintaining safety standards,
reducing risk to company, staff and customers and achieving industry standards. They
evaluate existing security systems to determine potential risks in all transport sectors such
as road and sea transport, and develop policies and procedures that minimise the risk to
properties, employees and computer systems.
