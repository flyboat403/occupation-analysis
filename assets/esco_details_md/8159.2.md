## Description

Braiding textile technicians perform operations related to setting up the braiding process.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **control textile process** 
- **draw sketches to develop textile articles using softwares** 
- **distinguish accessories** 
- **measure yarn count** 
- **use weft preparation technologies** 
- **develop specifications of technical textiles** 
- **distinguish fabrics**
