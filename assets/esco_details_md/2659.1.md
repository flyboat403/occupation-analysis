Description
Community artists research, plan, organise and lead artistic activities for people brought
together by a shared interest, capacity, environment or condition. They manage and
coordinate creative projects with local groups and individuals to foster their artistic
creativity and improve their quality of life. Community artists make the arts accessible to
the community they work for, and provide opportunities for participants to shape their
artistic programme.
