Description
Conceptual artists choose any material as an artistic tool or/and material to be presented
as an artistic experience to the public. Their work, belonging to the fine arts, can be two-
dimensional (drawing, painting, collage), three-dimensional (sculpture, installation) or four-
dimensional (moving images, performance).
