Description
Robotics engineering technicians collaborate with engineers in the development of robotic
devices and applications through a combination of mechanical engineering, electronic
engineering, and computer engineering. Robotics engineering technicians build, test,
install and calibrate robotic equipment.
Scope note
Excludes automation engineering technician.
