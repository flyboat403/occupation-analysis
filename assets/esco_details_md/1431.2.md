Description
Recreational facilities managers direct the operations of facilities that provide recreational
services such as gardens, spas, zoos, gambling and lottery facilities. They plan and
organise the daily operations of the related staff and facilities and ensure the organisation
follows the latest developments in its field. They coordinate the different departments of
the facility and manage the correct use of resources and budgets.
