Description
Actors/actresses play roles and parts on live stage performances, TV, radio, video, motion
picture productions, or other settings for entertainment or instruction. They use body
language (gestures and dancing) and voice (speech and singing) in order to present the
character or story according to the script, following the guidelines of a director.
