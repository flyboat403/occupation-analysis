Description
Performance production managers take care of a range of practical issues concerning the
production of a performance or entertainment event. They deal with matters ranging from
the recruitment of staff, procurement of materials and services, to freight, customs
coordination, telecommunications, labor relations, logistics, information
technology, government liaison, venue booking, scheduling, operations management,
mending delay problems and workplace safety.
