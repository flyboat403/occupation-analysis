Description
ICT research consultants perform targeted ICT research and provide a final report to the
client. They also use ICT tools to design questionnaires for surveys, analyse the results,
write reports, present the results and make recommendations based on the research
findings.
Scope note
Includes people performing outsourced activities. Excludes people performing in-house
research.
