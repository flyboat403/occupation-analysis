Description
Prison instructors educate legal offenders, including prisoners, on social rehabilitation and
correctional behaviour. They assist prisoners in gaining skills which could facilitate their
reintegration into society and increase their chances at finding employment after release.
Prison instructors analyse the students' individual learning needs, plan and prepare
teaching materials and sessions and update their learning records. They also ensure the
working area and materials are safe from danger and accounted for, and supervise the
students at all times.
