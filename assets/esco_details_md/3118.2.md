Description
Clothing CAD technicians use software to create design plans for clothing products. They
work in 2D design which is known as surface modelling, or 3D design which is called solid
modelling. They use surface modelling to draw a flat representation of the clothing
product. In solid modelling, they create a 3D display of a structure or component in order
to take a virtual look of the clothing product.
