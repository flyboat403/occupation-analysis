## Alternative Terms

- V-belt covering machine operative

## Description

V-belt coverers operate machines that cover belts with rubberised fabric. They cut the fabric after one revolution of the belt.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **cut rubberized fabrics** 
- **tend V-belt covering machine** 
- **press rubberized fabrics** 
- **label belts** 
- **cover V-belts with fabric** 
- **apply health and safety standards** 
- **position V-belts on covering machine** 
- **use personal protection equipment**
