Description
Disability support workers provide personal assistance and support to individuals of all
ages with disability conditions, either intellectual or physical disabilities. They work with
other health professionals to maximise individuals physical and mental well being. Their
duties include bathing, lifting, moving, dressing or feeding people with disability.
