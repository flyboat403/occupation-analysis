Description
Pneumatic systems technicians use blueprints and technical documentation to assemble
and install equipment operated by gas or air under pressure. They set up the systems
according to engineering specifications and test them to ensure good functioning order.
They may also perform maintenance and repair work on installed pneumatic equipment.
