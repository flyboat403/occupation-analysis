Description
Armoured car drivers drive the armoured car to transfer valuable items, such as money, to
different locations. They never leave the car. They work in cooperation with the armoured
car guards who deliver the valuables to their final recipients. Armoured car drivers ensure
vehicle security at all times by following company policies.
