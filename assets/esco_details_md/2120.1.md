Description
Actuarial consultants analyse, manage and provide guidance on financial impact of risks.
They can work in fields related to insurance, pension, investment, banking, healthcare etc.
Actuarial consultants apply technical and statistical models and theories to give strategic,
commercial, and financial advice.
