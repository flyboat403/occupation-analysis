Description
Biology teachers at secondary schools provide education to students, commonly children
and young adults, in a secondary school setting. They are usually subject teachers,
specialised and instructing in their own field of study, biology. They prepare lesson plans
and materials, monitor the students' progress, assist individually when necessary, and
evaluate the students' knowledge and performance on the subject of biology through
assignments, tests and examinations.
