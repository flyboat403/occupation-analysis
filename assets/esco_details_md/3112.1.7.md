Description
Fire protection technicians install and maintain fire protection equipment, such as fire
extinguishers, fire alarms, fire detection systems, or sprinkler systems in facilities to
ensure compliance to safety standards and protection from fire hazards. They inspect the
equipment to ensure its functionality, and perform repairs.
