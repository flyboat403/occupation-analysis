Description
Theme park technicians work to maintain and repair amusement park attractions. They
need a strong technical knowledge and have specialised knowledge of the rides they are
assigned to maintain. Theme park technicians usually keep records of the maintenance and
repairs performed as well as uptime and downtime for each serviced attraction. Attention
to safety is especially important in the maintenance and repair of amusement park rides.
