Description
Metalworking lathe operators set up and tend a metalworking lathe manually, which is
responsible for cutting metal to its desired size and shape by usage of a gear train or a
swapping gear that propels the main lead-screw at a variable speed ratio, thus rotating the
metal workpiece on its axis, facilitating the cutting process. They check the lathe
equipment for wear and handle the metal workpieces as they have been cut by the lathe.
Scope note
Excludes lathe and turning machine operator.
