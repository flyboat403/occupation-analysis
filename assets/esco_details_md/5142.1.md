Description
Aestheticians offer skin care treatments. They apply various facial treatments according to
their clients' needs and skin type, such as lotions, scrubs, peels and masks, in order to
maintain the skin healthy and attractive. Aestheticians may also give neck massages and
body treatments such as wraps. Aestheticians remove unwanted hair on different body
parts like eyebrows, upper lip or the bikini area. They perform facial massage and apply
make-up for various occasions.
