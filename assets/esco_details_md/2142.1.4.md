Description
Geological engineers apply geological knowledge for the assessment of sites, soils, slope
stability, sediments and other observable characteristics in the Earth. They integrate this
information in the planning and development of projects in those places. They assess and
answer questions concerning the geological characteristics of soils by performing research
and experiments on the sites aimed to be intervened.
