Description
Volunteer managers work across the non-profit sector to recruit, train, motivate and
supervise volunteers. They are in charge of designing volunteer assignments, recruiting
volunteers, reviewing the tasks undertaken and impact made, providing feedback and
managing their overall performance against the objectives of the organisation. Volunteer
coordinators might also manage online volunteering activities, sometimes known as cyber-
volunteering or e-volunteering.
