Description
Real estate managers handle and oversee the operational aspects of commercial or
residential properties such as private apartments, office buildings and retail stores. They
negotiate contracts for lease, identify and plan new real estate projects and construction of
new buildings by partnering with a developer to identify the appropriate site for new
buildings, coordinate the feasibility study for new constructions and supervise all the
administrative and technical aspects involved in expanding the business. They maintain the
premises and aim to increase its value. They hire, train and supervise personnel.
