Description
Composers create new music pieces in a variety of styles. They usually note down the
created music in musical notation. Composers may work independently or as part of a
group or ensemble. Many create pieces to support film, television, games or live
performances.
Scope note
Excludes sound artist, lyricist and music arranger.
