Description
Medical laboratory technology vocational teachers technology instruct students in their
specialised field of study, medical laboratory technology, which is predominantly practical
in nature. They provide theoretical instruction in service of the practical skills and
techniques the students must subsequently master for a medical laboratory technology-
related profession. Medical laboratory technology vocational teachers monitor the
students' progress, assist individually when necessary, and evaluate their knowledge and
performance on the subject of medical laboratory technology through assignments, tests
and examinations.
