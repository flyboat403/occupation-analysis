Description
Electronics engineers research, design, and develop electronic systems such as circuits,
semiconductor devices, and equipment that use electricity as a source of power. They work
with capacitators, transistors, diodes or resistors to create electronic circuits and
applications of use in fields such as telecommunications, acoustics, instruments, and
control.
