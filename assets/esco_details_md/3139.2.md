Description
Industrial robot controllers operate and monitor industrial robots used in automation
processes to perform various manufacturing activities such as lifting, welding and
assembling. They ensure that the machines are working correctly and in sync with other
industrial robots, maintain and repair defective parts, assess risks and perform tests.
