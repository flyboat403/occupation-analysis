## Alternative Terms

- airline pilot
- commercial pilot
- captain
- pilot of commercial aircraft
- pilot of commercial aeroplanes

## Description

Commercial pilots navigate flight of fixed-wing and multi-engine aircrafts for the transport of passengers and cargo.
