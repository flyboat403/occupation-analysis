Description
Financial controllers handle all tasks related to the budgeting and accounting aspects of a
company or organisation. They implement and ensure compliance with internal financial
and accounting procedures, and prepare documentation for external audits. They collect
information related to financial statements such as assets, liabilities, equity, and cash flow
in order to assess the company's financial position to prepare annual budgets and
forecasts.
