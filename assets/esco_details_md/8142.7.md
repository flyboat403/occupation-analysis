Description
Injection moulding operators operate and monitor injection moulding machines, in order to
cast products from thermoplastic materials. They regulate the temperature, pressure, and
volume of plastic, according to pre-defined specifications. Injection moulding operators
also remove finished products and cut-away excess material, using a knife or other hand
tools.
Scope note
Includes people producing jewellery and bijouterie.
