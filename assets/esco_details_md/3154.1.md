Description
Air traffic controllers assist pilots by providing information concerning the height, speed
and course. They assist pilots in order to facilitate a safe take off and landing of aircrafts.
They are responsible for maintaining a secure and orderly movement of aircraft along
major air routes up in the sky and around airports. They control air traffic in and within
vicinity of airports according to established procedures and policies to prevent collisions
and to minimise delays arising from traffic congestion.
