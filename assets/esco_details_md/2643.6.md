Description
Translators transcribe written documents from one or more languages to another ensuring
that the message and nuances therein remain in the translated material. They translate
material backed up by an understanding of it, which can include commercial and industrial
documentation, personal documents, journalism, novels, creative writing, and scientific
texts delivering the translations in any format.
