Description
Agricultural inspectors monitor agricultural operations in farms and other agricultural
facilities. They inspect activities such as health and safety measures, costs and production
processes to ensure that workers and their activities comply with proper legislation and
standards. Agricultural inspectors also analyse and report on their findings.
