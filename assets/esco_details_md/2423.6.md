Description
Recruitment consultants provide the suitable candidates to employers according to the
specific job profile requested. They perform testing and interviewing with job seekers,
shortlist few candidates to present to the employers and match candidates to appropriate
jobs. Recruitment consultants maintain relationships with employers to offer their services
on a more long-term basis.
