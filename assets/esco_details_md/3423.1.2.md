Description
Outdoor animators are responsible for planning and organising outdoor activities. They
may at times be involved in aspects of administration, front office tasks and tasks related to
the activity base and maintenance of equipment. The workplace of an outdoor animator is
mostly “in the field”, but can also take place indoors.
