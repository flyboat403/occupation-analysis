Description
Care home workers provide domiciliary services to vulnerable adults including frail elderly
or disabled people who are living with physical impairment or convalescing, following a
specific plan to provide day-to-day care to clients. They look after the physical and mental
wellbeing of clients by providing them social care. These services could be developed in
residential homes, care homes or in the patient's home. In relation to this last case, they
aim to improve patients' lives in the community and assure patients can live safely and
independently in their own home.
