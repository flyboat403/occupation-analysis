Description
Security guard supervisors monitor and oversee the activities of guards who protect
properties from vandalism acts and theft. They assign areas to be patrolled by guards on a
regular basis, transfer the individual caught trespassing to police custody and develop
safety plans and drills for the buildings and employees under their supervision.
