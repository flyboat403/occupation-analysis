## Description

Pyrotechnics drying room attendants monitor and maintain the drying room, making sure the pyrotechnics are dried and stored within the correct parameters and specifications.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **adjust drying process to goods** 
- **control pyrotechnics stock** 
- **control temperature** 
- **handle flammable substances** 
- **monitor end-product drying process** 
- **oversee stock quality control** 
- **store pyrotechnical materials** 
- **tend pyrotechnics drying room** 
- **use chemical analysis equipment** 

### Knowledge

- **explosives** 
- **fire prevention procedures** 
- **hazardous waste storage**
