Description
Clothing quality inspectors inspect manufactured components and ready-made garments in
order to classify them according to their quality by ensuring compliance with quality
standards and identifying defects or deviations from specifications. They inspect and test
products, parts and materials for conformity with specifications and standards. They
ensure all work produced meets or exceeds the department's quality standards.
