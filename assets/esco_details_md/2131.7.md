Description
Pharmacologists study the manner in which drugs and medicaments interact with
organisms, living systems, and their parts (i.e. cells, tissues, or organs). Their research
aims at identifying substances that can be ingested by humans and that exert adequate
biochemical functions for curing illnesses.
