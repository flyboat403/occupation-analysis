4223.1.1
Description
Emergency medical dispatchers respond to urgent calls made to the control center, take
information about the emergency situation, the address and other details and dispatch the
nearest ambulance or paramedic helicopter.
Scope note
Excludes people performing patients treatment. Excludes people performing assistance in
medical treatment.
