Description
Telecommunications engineers design, build, test and maintain telecommunication systems
and networks, including radio and broadcasting equipment.
They analyse customer needs and requirements, ensure that the equipment meets
regulations, and prepare and present reports and proposals on telecommunication-related
problems. Telecommunications engineers design and oversee the service delivery in all it's
phases, supervising the installation and use of telecommunications equipment and
facilities, preparing documentation and providing training for company staff once new
equipment has been installed.
Scope note
Excludes people performing managerial activities.
