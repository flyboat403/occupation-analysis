Description
Musical conductors lead ensembles of musicians directing them during rehersals and
helping them atteining their best performance. They can work with a variety of ensembles
such as choirs and orchestras. Musical conductors adjust the tempo (speed), rhythm,
dynamics (loud or soft) and articulation (smooth or detached) of the music using gestures
and sometimes dancing to motivate the musicians to play according to the music sheet.
