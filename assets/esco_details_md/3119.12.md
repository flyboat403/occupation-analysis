Description
Photonics engineering technicians collaborate with engineers in the development of
photonic systems or components, usually in the form of optical equipment, such as lasers,
lenses, and fibre optic equipment. Photonics engineering technicians build, test, install and
calibrate optical equipment. They read blueprint and other technical drawings to develop
testing and calibrating procedures.
