Description
Dance teachers instruct students in a recreational context in the various dance genres and
forms, such as ballet, jazz, tap, ballroom, hip-hop, latin, folk dance etc. They provide
students with a notion of dance history and repertoire, but mainly focus on a practice-
based approach in their courses, in which they assist students in experimenting with and
mastering different dance and dramatic expression styles and techniques and encourage
them to develop their own style. They cast, choreograph and produce performances, and
coordinate the technical production and the set, props and costume usage on stage.
