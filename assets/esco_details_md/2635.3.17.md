Description
Mental health social workers assist and provide counselling to people with mental,
emotional, or substance abuse problems. They focus on providing personalised support to
cases and monitor the recovery process of their clients by providing therapy, crisis
intervention, client advocacy and education. Mental health social workers may contribute
to mental health services improvement and mental health outcomes for citizens.
