Description
Construction general contractors take on the responsibility to deliver a construction
project. They participate on bid processes for construction projects and hire
subcontractors to deliver the different stages of the construction process from beginning to
completion. They make sure subcontractors hold up their part of the agreement and work
in the construction site to make sure the project is delivered in time and according to
agreed standards.
