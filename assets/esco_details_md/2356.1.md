Description
ICT trainers conduct training-needs analysis and design programs to train students in the
use of software packages and information systems accordingly. They produce and update
existing training materials (content and method), deliver effective training in classroom,
online or informally, monitor, evaluate and report effectiveness of training. They maintain
and update expertise on specialised ICT subjects and evaluate and report on students'
performance.
