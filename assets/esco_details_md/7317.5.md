Description
Wicker furniture makers choose and prepare materials such as softened rattan or willow
branches in order to produce wicker furniture such as chairs, tables and couches. They use
hand, power or machine tools to cut, bend and weave materials to create the desired
objects. Finally, they treat the surface to ensure a finished look and protect it from
corrosion and fire using waxes, lacquers and other coatings.
