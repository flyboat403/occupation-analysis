Description
Non-destructive testing specialists carry out testing of vehicles, vessels, other
manufactured objects, and construction structures without having to damage them. They
use special equipment such as X-ray, ultrasound, radiographics, or infrared instruments to
perform testing activities and report based on the observed results.
