Description
Toilet attendants clean and maintain toilet facilities in accordance with company standards
and policies. They use cleaning equipment to clean mirrors, floors, toilets and sinks. They
perform the cleaning activities before, during and after operational service hours. Toilet
attendants refill the facility with supplies as needed and maintain records of their daily
operations.
