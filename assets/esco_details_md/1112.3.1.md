Description
Ambassadors represent their own government in foreign countries for diplomatic and
peace-keeping purposes. They deal with political negotiation between the country of origin
and the country where they are stationed and ensure the protection of citizens from their
home nation in the nation they are stationed. They facilitate communication between the
two nations and perform advisory functions to the home government to help develop
foreign policy.
