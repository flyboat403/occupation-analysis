Description
Water plant technicians maintain and repair water treatment and supply equipment in a
water plant. They ensure the provision of clean water by measuring the water quality,
ensuring it is filtered and treated correctly, and maintaining distribution systems.
Scope note
Excludes water treatment systems operator water treatment systems operator.
