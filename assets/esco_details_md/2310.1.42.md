Description
University teaching assistants are graduate students or recent graduates employed on a
temporary contract at a university or a college for teaching-related responsibilities. They
assist the professor, lecturer or teacher of the specific course they are in charge of in the
preparation of lectures and of exams, grading papers and exams and leading review and
feedback sessions for the students.
