Description
Surface grinding machine operators set up and tend surface grinding machines designed to
apply abrasive processes in order to remove small amounts of excess material and
smoothen metal workpieces by an abrasive grinding wheel, or wash grinder, rotating on a
horizontal or vertical axis.
Alternative Labels
back grinding machine operator
filling and grinding machine operator
