Description
Automotive electricians install, maintain and repair electrical and electronic systems in
motor vehicles such as air conditioning systems, lamps, radios, heating systems, batteries,
electrical wiring and alternators. They use diagnostic testing equipment to inspect vehicles
and find faults. To do repair work, they use hand tools and specialised electrical
instruments and machines.
