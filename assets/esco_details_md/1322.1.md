Description
Mine managers control, direct, plan and coordinate mining production activities. They have
statutory responsibility for safety and are also responsible for environmental impact. They
oversee the acquisition, installation, maintenance and storage of mining plant and
equipment. They lead and manage according to the organisation's code of conduct.
