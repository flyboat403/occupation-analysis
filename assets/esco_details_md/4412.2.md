Description
Postmen/Postwomen deliver mails and parcel posts to residences and businesses. They
perform mail delivery and collect signatures from the recipients. They perform other duties
in connection with mail services from post offices or related organisations.
Scope note
Includes people using bicycles and motorcycles.
