Description
Dietetic technicians assist dietitians in planning nutritional programmes and in planning
menus for patients as part of an overall treatment plan under supervision of a dietitian.
Under supervision of a dietitian, dietetic technicians assist dietitians in patient reviews,
enteral feeding discharge planning, and clinical auditing which includes nutritional
screening.
