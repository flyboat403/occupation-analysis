Description
Sustainability managers are responsible for ensuring the sustainability of business
processes. They provide assistance in the design and implementation of plans and
measures to ensure that the manufacturing processes and products comply with given
environmental regulations and social responsibility standards and they monitor and report
on the implementation of sustainability strategies within the company supply chain and
business process. They analyse issues linked to manufacturing processes, use of materials,
waste reduction, energy efficiency and products traceability to improve environmental and
social impacts and integrate sustainability aspects into the company culture.
Scope note
Does not include corporate responsibility managers and enterprise development workers.
