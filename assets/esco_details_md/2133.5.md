Description
Ecologists carry out assessments of the health and distribution of organisms, namely
people, plants, and animals, and the relationship between organisms and their
environment. Ecologists usually have a specialisation area, e.g. freshwater, marine,
terrestrial, fauna, and flora about which they conduct research and perform related tasks.
Scope note
Work includes the analysis and testing of living organisms, the development and
application of processes resulting from research in areas such as natural resource
management, environmental protection and microbiology.
