Description
Quarry engineers analyse which extraction methods such as excavating, drilling and
blasting are most suited to extract raw materials from the ground. They develop plans
before a new quarry is opened, assessing if the quarry is profitable. Quarry engineers
manage the everyday operations in a quarry, create and maintain progress reports, oversee
the staff, ensure health and safety and assess the environmental impact a quarry has on its
environment.
