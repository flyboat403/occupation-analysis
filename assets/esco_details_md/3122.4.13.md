Description
Plastic and rubber products manufacturing supervisors manage and coordinate the
activities of personnel involved in plastic or rubber products manufacturing, making sure
the production is efficiently, safely and cost-effectively processed. They are responsible for
the installation of new production lines and for the provision of trainings.
