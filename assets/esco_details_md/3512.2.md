Description
ICT help desk managers monitor the delivery of technical support services to clients
according to the predefined deadlines. They plan and organise the user support actions
and troubleshoot ICT problems and issues. ICT help desk managers supervise the help
desk team ensuring that the customers get the appropriate feedback and support. They
also participate in developing customer service guidelines and in reinforcing the team.
