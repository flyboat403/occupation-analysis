Description
Sensory scientists carry out sensory analysis in order to compose or improve flavours and
fragrances for the food, beverage and cosmetics industry. They base their flavour and
fragrance development on sensory and consumer research. Sensory scientists carry out
research and analyse statistical data to meet customers expectations.
