Description
Visual arts teachers instruct students in various styles of visual arts, such as drawing,
painting, and sculpting, in a recreational context. They provide students with an overview
of art history, but primarily utilise a practice-based approach in their courses, in which
they assist students to experiment with and master different artistic techniques, and
encourage them to develop their own style.
