Description
Spa attendants interact with guests at the reception desk to provide indications and
explain the available services in the spa center. They are also in charge of cleaning the spa
rooms after use, maintaining storage area cleanliness and restocking clean towels. Spa
attendants monitor the security of the facility and are responsible for selling the products
and services available such as treatment packages.
