Description
Cultural facilities managers direct the operations of facilities that provide cultural services
such as theatre, museums and concert halls. They plan and organise the daily operations of
the related staff and facilities and ensure the organisation follows the latest developments
in its field. They coordinate the different departments of the facility and manage the
correct use of resources, policies and budgets.
