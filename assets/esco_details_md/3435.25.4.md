Description
Stage machinists manipulate sets and other elements in a performance based on the
artistic or creative concept, in interaction with the performers. Their work is influenced by
and influences the results of other operators. Therefore, the stage machinists work closely
together with the designers, operators and performers. Stage machinists prepare and
perform the setup, execute changeovers and operate manual fly bar systems. Their work is
based on plans, instructions and calculations.
