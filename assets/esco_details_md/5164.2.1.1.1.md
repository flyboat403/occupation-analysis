Description
Guide dog instructors train dogs to be responsible in guiding blind people to travel
effectively. They plan the training sessions, match guide dogs with their clients and ensure
overall routine care of the training dogs. Guide dog instructors also provide advice to the
blind people in the use of techniques that facilitate dog's travel skills and mobility.
