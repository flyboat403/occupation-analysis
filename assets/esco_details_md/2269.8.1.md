Description
Diagnostic radiographers plan, prepare and perform diagnostic imaging examinations and
post processing, with a wide range of equipment and techniques using X-rays, strong
magnetic fields or Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and high frequency sound
(Ultrasound).
