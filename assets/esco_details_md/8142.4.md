Description
Fiberglass laminators mould fiberglass materials to form hulls and boat decks. They read
blueprints and use hand and power tools to cut the composite materials. They apply waxes
and lacquers, and prepare surfaces for the placement of fiberglass mats. They use resin-
saturated fiberglass to bond wood reinforcing strips to cabin structures and decks. They
also prepare materials exposing those to the right temperature. They check finished
products for defects and make sure they comply with the specifications.
Scope note
Includes people performing the manufacture of motor vehicles, aircrafts and wind turbines.
