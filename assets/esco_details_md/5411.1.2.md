Description
Marine firefighters are responsible for emergency response in case of a fire outbreak or
other hazardous situations in marine environments. They actively respond to contain fires
and the outbreak of other hazardous on vessels, docks and other marine facilities. They
ensure the marine installation complies with health and safety regulations. They also
regulate the clean-up of the scene and assess the damage.
