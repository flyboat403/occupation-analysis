Description
Automated cable vehicle controllers operate systems and control boards keeping under the
control the operations of a varied array of modes of transport operated by cable. They can
operate aerial cabins, telphers, funiculars, etc. They ensure continuous operations and
intervene in the operations when unforeseen situations occur.
