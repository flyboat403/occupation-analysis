Description
Probation officers supervise offenders after their release, or who were sentenced to
penalties outside of incarceration. They write reports providing advice on the offender's
sentence and analysis concerning possibilities of reoffense. They aid the offenders during
the rehabilitation and reintegration process and ensure the offenders carry out their
community service sentence when necessary.
