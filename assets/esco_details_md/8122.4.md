Description
Cylindrical grinder operators set up and tend cylindrical grinding machines designed to
apply abrasive processes in order to remove small amounts of excess material and
smoothen metal workpieces by multiple abrasive grinding wheels with diamond teeth as a
cutting device for very precise and light cuts, as the workpiece is fed past it and formed
into a cylinder.
