Description
Industrial firefighters are responsible for emergency response in case of a fire outbreak or
other hazardous situations in industrial areas or facilities. They actively respond to contain
fires and the outbreak of other hazardous substances to protect the industrial crews and
premises. They ensure the industrial facility complies with health and safety regulations.
They also regulate the clean-up of the scene and assess the damage.
