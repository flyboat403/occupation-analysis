Description
Embroiderers puch designs and decorate textile surfaces by hand or by using an
embroidery machine. They apply a range of traditional stitching techniques to produce
intricate designs on clothing, accessories, and home decor items. Professional
embroiderers combine traditional sewing skills with current software programs to design
and construct embellishments on an item.
Scope note
Excludes people performing patients treatment. Excludes people performing assistance in
medical treatment.
