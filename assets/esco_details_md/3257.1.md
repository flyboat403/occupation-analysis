Description
Environmental health inspectors carry out investigations to ensure that areas,
organisations and companies comply with environmental and public health legislation.
They evaluate environmental complaints, provide reports on their findings and work to
prevent future hazards or noncompliance with current policies. Environmental health
inspectors perform consultations to promote public health and safety.
