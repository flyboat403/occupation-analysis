Description
Mattress makers form mattresses by creating pads and coverings. They tuft mattresses by
hand and cut, spread and attach the padding and cover material over the innerspring
assemblies.
Scope note
Excludes people producing inflatable rubber mattresses and rubber waterbed mattresses.
