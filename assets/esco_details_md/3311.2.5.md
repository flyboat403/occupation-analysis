Description
Stock brokers act on behalf of their individual or institutional clients in order to buy and
sell stocks and other securities. They are in close contact with their clients and ensure that
what they buy or sell through the stock exchange market is according to their clients'
wishes. Stock brokers undertake analyst research to make recommendations to their
clients and expand their client base through various methods.
