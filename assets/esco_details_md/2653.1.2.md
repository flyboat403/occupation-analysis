Description
Dance répétiteurs assist conductors and choreographers in directing rehearsals and
guiding the artists in the rehearsal process. Regardless of their nature and scope, a
répétiteur's actions are, from an ethical and practical standpoint, based on a commitment
to respect the integrity of the work.
Scope note
Excludes people working in an educational work context.
