Description
Survey enumerators perform interviews and fill in forms in order to collect the data
provided by interviewees. They can collect information by phone, mail, personal visits or on
the street. They conduct and help the interviewees administer the information that the
interviewer is interested in having, usually related to demographic information for
governmental statistical purposes.
