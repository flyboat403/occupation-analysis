Description
Integration engineers develop and implement solutions which coordinate applications
across the enterprise or its units and departments. They evaluate existing components or
systems to determine integration requirements and ensure that the final solutions meet
organisational needs. They reuse components when possible and assist management in
taking decisions. They perform ICT system integration troubleshooting.
Scope note
Includes people performing managerial activities.
