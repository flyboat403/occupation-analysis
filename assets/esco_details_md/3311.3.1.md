Description
Energy traders sell or buy shares of energy, sometimes from different sources. They
analyse the energy market and investigate trends in prices to decide when to buy or sell
shares and ensure the most profit. They make calculations, and write reports on energy
trades procedures, and make predictions on the development of the market.
