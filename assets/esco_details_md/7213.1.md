Description
Boilermakers operate a variety of equipment and machinery to create, repipe and retube
hot water and steam boilers, producing them in all steps of the production process. They
cut, gouge and shape the metal sheets and tubes for the boilers to size, using oxy-acetylene
gas torches, assemble them by shielded metal arc welding, gas metal arc welding or gas
tungsten arc welding, and finish them by the approprate machine tools, power tools and
coating.
