## Description

Nonwoven textile technicians perform operations related to setting up non-woven processes.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **control textile process** 
- **develop specifications of technical textiles** 
- **distinguish accessories** 
- **distinguish fabrics** 
- **maintain work standards** 
- **manufacture non-woven filament products** 
- **measure yarn count**
