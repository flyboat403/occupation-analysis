## Alternative Terms

- producer of podcasts
- podcaster

## Description

Podcast producers supervise the entire production process of a podcast. This process involves planning the episodes, supporting audio recording and editing, production tasks and publishing the final version of the product. They also develop promotion and marketing strategies based on podcast market trends and target audience.
