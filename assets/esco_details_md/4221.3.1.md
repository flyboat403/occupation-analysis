Description
Railway sales agents provide service to customers who visit the ticket counter. They
provide information, handle ticket reservations, sales and refunds. They also carry out
clerical duties such as maintaining the daily ticket sales balance sheet. They handle
requests for seat reservations and examine diagram charts of each car on train to verify
the available space on the specified train.
Scope note
Excludes people working in road and urban transport industries collecting fares.
