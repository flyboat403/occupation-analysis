Description
Advertising managers perform the implementation of the advertising initiatives planned in
the strategic marketing plan. They organise and prepare the resources needed to launch
advertising campaigns and operations in advertising agencies. They prepare and align the
communication channels, negotiate contracts, and ensure that operations adhere to
budgets.
