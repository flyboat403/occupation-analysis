Description
Health and safety inspectors investigate the implementation and application of labour
standards and policies in a workplace. They advise employers as well as employees on the
improvement of policy and legislation implementation, ensure that the law is adhered to,
and that matters of equality and labour rights are respected. They write reports and
communicate with authorities.
