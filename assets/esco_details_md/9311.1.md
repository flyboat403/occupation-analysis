## Alternative Terms

- mining assistant

## Description

Mining assistants perform routine duties in mining and quarrying operations. They assist the miners with maintaining equipment, with laying pipes, cables and tunnels, and with removing wast.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **dispose of non-hazardous waste** 
- **perform minor repairs to equipment** 
- **maintain records of mining operations** 
- **conduct inter-shift communication** 
- **handle mining plant waste** 
- **troubleshoot** 
- **address problems critically** 
- **work ergonomically** 
- **clean waste materials from machines** 

### Knowledge

- **excavation techniques**
