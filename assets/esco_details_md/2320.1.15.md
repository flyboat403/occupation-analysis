Description
Hospitality vocational teachers instruct students in their specialised field of study,
hospitality, which is predominantly practical in nature. They provide theoretical instruction
in service of the practical skills and techniques the students must subsequently master for
a hospitality-related profession, such as hotel receptionist or housekeeper. Hospitality
vocational teachers monitor the students' progress, assist individually when necessary, and
evaluate their knowledge and performance on the subject of hospitality through
assignments, tests and examinations.
