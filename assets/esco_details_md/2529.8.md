Description
Cybersecurity risk managers identify, analyse, assess, estimate and mitigate cybersecurity-
related risks of ICT infrastructures such as systems or services. They manage these aspects
by planning risk analysis, applying, reporting, assessing, communicating, and treating
them. They establish a risk management strategy for the organisation and ensure that risks
remain at an acceptable level for the organisation by selecting mitigation actions and
controls.
