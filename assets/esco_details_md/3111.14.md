Description
Soil surveying technicians analyse soil by carrying out technical surveying tasks, using soil
surveying techniques. They focus on the process of classifying soil types and other soil
properties. Soil surveying technicians operate surveying equipment and utilise programs to
retrieve and interpret relevant data, and perform computations as required.
