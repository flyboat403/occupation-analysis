Description
Dangerous goods safety advisers inspect and make transport recommendations in line with
the European regulations regarding the transport of dangerous goods. They may advise on
the transport of dangerous goods by road, rail, sea and air. Dangerous goods safety
advisers also prepare safety reports and investigate safety infringements. They provide
individuals with the procedures and instructions to follow during the loading, unloading
and transporting of these goods.
