Description
Food service vocational teachers for food service instruct students in their specialised field
of study, food service, which is predominantly practical in nature. They provide theoretical
instruction in service of the practical skills and techniques the students must subsequently
master for a food service-related profession. Food service vocational teachers monitor the
students' progress, assist individually when necessary, and evaluate their knowledge and
performance on the subject of food service through assignments, tests and examinations.
