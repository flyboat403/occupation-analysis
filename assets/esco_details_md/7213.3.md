Description
Coppersmiths craft and repair items made of non-ferrous metals such as copper, brass and
similar materials. They shape and form the raw materials into objects of practical or
artistic purpose using smithing tools. Professional coppersmiths create detailed and highly
technical devices using appropriate smithing techniques.
