Description
Warehouse operators for clothing are in charge of storing textile fabrics, accessories and
components for clothing production. They ensure that all the components necessary for the
production of clothing are ready to use in the production chain by classifying and
registering the purchased component, forecasting purchases and distributing them across
different departments.
