## Description

Air pollution analysts conduct field and lab tests to examine the pollution of air in different areas. They also identify sources of pollution.

## Scope Note

Includes people working with specialists such as engineers and <a resource="http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/11d72bd6-779a-4643-9962-313833740094" typeOf="http://data.europa.eu/esco/model#Occupation" href="http://data.europa.eu/esco/occupation/11d72bd6-779a-4643-9962-313833740094"><span property="http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#label" xml:lang="en">environmental scientists</span></a>.
