Description
Financial managers handle all the matters in reference to the finance and investments of a
company. They manage financial operations of companies such as the assets, liabilities,
equity and cash flow aiming to maintain the financial health of the company and operative
viability. Financial managers evaluate the strategic plans of the company in financial
terms, maintain transparent financial operations for taxation and auditing bodies, and
create the financial statements of the company at the end of the fiscal year.
