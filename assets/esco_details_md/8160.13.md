Description
Cellar operators take charge of fermentation and maturation tanks. They control
fermentation process of wort inoculated with yeast. They tend equipment that cools and
adds yeast to wort as to produce beer. For the purpose, they control the flow of
refrigeration that goes through cool coils regulating the temperature of hot wort in the
tanks.
