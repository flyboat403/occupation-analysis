Description
ICT usability testers ensure compliance with requirements and strive for optimal usability
within the software engineering cycle phases (analysis, design, implementation, and
deployment). They also work closely with users (analysts) to research for and document
user profiles, analyse tasks, workflows, and user scenarios.
