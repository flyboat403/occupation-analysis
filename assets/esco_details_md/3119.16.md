Description
Quality engineering technicians work with quality engineers or managers to analyse and
solve quality problems and improve productivity. They examine machines for imperfections
and inspect products to make sure they meet the standards. They also provide personnel
with training in inspection techniques and prepare inspection plans.
Scope note
Excludes people performing the evaluation of the production process.
