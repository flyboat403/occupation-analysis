Description
Event managers plan and oversee events such as festivals, conferences, ceremonies,
cultural events, exhibitions, formal parties, concerts, or conventions. They organise every
stage of the events planning the venues, staff, suppliers, media, insurances all within the
allocated budgetary and time limits. Event managers ensure the legal obligations are
followed and the expectations of the target audience are met. They work together with the
marketing team in promoting the event, searching new clients and gathering constructive
feedback after the events took place.
