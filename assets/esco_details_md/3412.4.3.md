Description
Child welfare workers provide early intervention and support to children and their families
in order to improve their social and psychological functioning. They aim to maximise the
family well-being and protect children from abuse and neglect. They advocate for children
so that their rights are respected within and outside the family. They may assist single
parents or find foster homes for abandoned or abused children.
