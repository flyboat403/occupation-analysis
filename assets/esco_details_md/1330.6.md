Description
ICT product managers analyse and define current and target status for ICT products,
services or solutions. They estimate the cost effectiveness, points of risk, opportunities,
strengths and weaknesses of products or services provided. ICT product managers create
structured plans and establish time scales and milestones, ensuring optimisation of
activities and resources.
