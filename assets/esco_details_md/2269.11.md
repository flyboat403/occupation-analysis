Description
Osteopaths provide therapeutic treatment of disorders in the musculoskeletal system to
patients with physical issues such as back pain, joint pain and digestive disorders. They
mainly use manipulation of the body tissues, touch, stretching and massage techniques to
relieve the patients` pain and promote a healthy lifestyle.
