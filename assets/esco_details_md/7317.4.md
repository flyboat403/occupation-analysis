Description
Toymakers create or reproduce hand-made objects for sale and exhibition made of various
materials such as plastic, wood and textile. They develop, design and sketch the object,
select the materials and cut, shape and process the materials as necessary and apply
finishes. In addition, toymakers maintain and repair all types of toys, including mechanical
ones. They identify defects in toys, replace damaged parts and restore their functionality.
