Description
Business lecturers are subject professors, teachers, or lecturers who instruct students who
have obtained an upper secondary education diploma in their own specialised field of
study, business, which is predominantly academic in nature. They work with their
university research assistants and university teaching assistants
for the preparation of lectures and of exams, grading papers and exams and leading review
and feedback sessions for the students. They also conduct academic research in their field
of business, publish their findings and liaise with other university colleagues.
