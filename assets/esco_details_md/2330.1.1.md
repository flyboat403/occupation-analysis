Description
Art teachers at secondary schools provide education to students, commonly children and
young adults, in a secondary school setting. They are usually subject teachers, specialised
and instructing in their own field of study, art. They prepare lesson plans and materials,
monitor the students' progress, assist individually when necessary, and evaluate the
students' knowledge and performance on the subject of art through assignments, tests and
examinations.
