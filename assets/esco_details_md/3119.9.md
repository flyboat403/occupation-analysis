Description
Leather goods manufacturing technicians perform a wide range of activities and tasks
related to the leather goods manufacturing process including cutting, closing and finishing,
according to pre-defined quality criteria, resulting from the direct relationship with the
costumer. They use manual techniques supported by simple traditional equipment to
produce exclusive models or very small orders.
