Description
Production designers are responsible for the complete look (style, colouring and locations)
of television programmes, series, motion pictures and commercials. They create the visual
concept for the entire production such as set design, lighting, costumes and camera
angles. Production designers work together with the director, designers and oversee the
art department. They also create sketches, drawings, do colouring and location research
and advise props and stage settings to the director.
