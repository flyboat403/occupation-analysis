Description
Shunters move shunting units with or without wagons or groups of wagons in order to
build trains. They manage the driving of locomotives and are involved in switching wagons,
making or splitting up trains in shunting yards or sidings. They operate according to the
technical features, such as controlling movement via a remote control device.
