Description
Midwives assist women in childbirth by providing the necessary support, care and advice
during pregnancy, labour and the postpartum period, conduct births and provide care for
the new-born. They advise on health, preventive measures, preparation for parenthood,
detection of complications in mother and child, accessing of medical care, promoting
normal birth and carrying out emergency measures.
