Description
Public administration managers direct, monitor and evaluate the implementation of
government policies. They supervise staff and manage resources used for implementation,
and write reports on the implementation process. They also communicate with government
officials and the public to inform them on the policies. Public administration managers may
also participate in the design and creation of public policies.
