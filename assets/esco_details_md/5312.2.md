Description
Primary school teaching assistants provide instructional and practical support to primary
school teachers. They reinforce instruction with students in need of extra attention and
prepare the materials the teacher needs in class. They also perform clerical work, monitor
the students' learning progress and behaviour and supervise the students with and without
the head teacher present.
