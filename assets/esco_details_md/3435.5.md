Description
Body artists decorate clients' skin temporarily or permanently. They use various techniques
such as tattooing or piercing. Body artists follow clients' preferences in terms of tattoo's or
piercing's design and body surface and apply it safely. They also advise on methods to
avoid infection following the procedures on clients' bodies.
