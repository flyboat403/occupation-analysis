Description
Video and motion picture directors are responsible for the overall production of a movie or
television program. They edit and translate the script into audiovisual images. Video and
motion picture directors supervise and manage the film crew. They convey their creative
vision on the actors, audio and video equipment operators, lighting technicians, etc and
direct them. Video and motion picture directors also supervise the editing of the footage.
