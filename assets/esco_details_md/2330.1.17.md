Description
Religious education teachers at secondary schools provide education to students,
commonly children and young adults, in a secondary school setting. They are usually
subject teachers, specialised and instructing in their own field of study, religion. They
prepare lesson plans and materials, monitor the students' progress, assist individually
when necessary, and evaluate the students' knowledge and performance on the subject of
religion through assignments, tests and examinations.
