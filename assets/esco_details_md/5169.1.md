## Description

Club host/hostess welcome customers to the club. They ensure that clients are well looked after and satisfied.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **assist VIP guests** 
- **assist customers** 
- **assist guest departure** 
- **deal with aggressive behaviour** 
- **enforce regulations of selling alcoholic beverages to minors** 
- **greet guests** 
- **handle customer complaints**
