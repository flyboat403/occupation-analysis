Description
Cosmetic chemists develop formulas in order to create and test new cosmetic products and
improve existing cosmetic products such as perfumes and fragrances, lipstick, waterproof
lotions and makeup, hair dye, soaps and detergents with special properties, topical
medicines or health supplements.
