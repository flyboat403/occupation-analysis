Description
Homelessness workers provide on-the-spot assistance, counselling and advice to people
who have housing problems or live on the streets. They present them with services
available to homeless people starting from hostel vacancies to financial aid services. They
may have to cope with persons with mental health problems, addictions or victims of
domestic or sexual abuse.
