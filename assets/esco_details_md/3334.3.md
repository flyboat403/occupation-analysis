Description
Real estate agents administer the sales or letting process of residential, commercial
properties or land on behalf of their clients. They investigate the property's condition and
assess its value in order to offer the best price to their clients. They negotiate, compose a
sales contract or a rental contract and liaise with third parties in order to realize the stated
objectives during transactions. They undertake research to determine the legality of a
property sale before it is sold and make sure the transaction is not subject to any disputes
or restrictions.
