Description
Sport administrators act in a middle management role within sport organisations at all
levels, in any sport or country in Europe (e.g. sport clubs, federations, and local
authorities). They carry out organisational tasks across a wide range of functions in
accordance with the strategy and policies set by management, boards of directors and
committees. They play a crucial role in the overall delivery of sport and their work in sport
organisations directly impact the unlocking of the potential of the sector in Europe towards
health, social inclusion and economy.
