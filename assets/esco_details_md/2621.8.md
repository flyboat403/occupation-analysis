Description
Museum scientists perform and/or manage the curatorial, preparatory and clerical work in
general museums, botanical gardens, art galleries, fine arts related collections, aquariums
or similar areas. They manage the collections of natural, historical and anthropological
material that is educational, scientific or aesthetic in purpose.
