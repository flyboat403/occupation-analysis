Description
Materials handlers execute the handling and storage of materials through activities such as
loading, unloading and moving articles in a warehouse or storage room. They work
according to orders to inspect materials and provide documentation for the handling of
items. Materials handlers also manage inventory and ensure the safe disposal of waste.
