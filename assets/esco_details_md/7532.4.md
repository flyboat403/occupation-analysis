## Alternative Terms

- digital pattern maker
- digital prototype operator
- pattern making machine operator
- digital patternmaker
- digital prototype technician

## Description

Digital prototypers transform paper pattern into digital form using special computer software. They operate and monitor machines which make different products related to clothing.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **use 3D scanners for clothing** 
- **analyse scanned data of the body** 
- **create patterns for garments** 
- **draw sketches to develop textile articles using softwares** 
- **prepare production prototypes** 
- **use pattern-cutting softwares** 
- **operate garment manufacturing machines** 
- **coordinate manufacturing production activities** 
- **grade patterns for wearing apparel**
