Description
ICT information and knowledge managers contribute to the definition of organisational
information strategy and apply information and knowledge creation, editing, storage, and
distribution policies. They manage the maintenance and evolution of structured and
unstructured information. They create digital structures to enable exploitation and
optimisation of information and knowledge, manage data analysis and enable business
intelligence.
