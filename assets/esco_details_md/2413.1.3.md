Description
Mergers and acquisitions analysts oversee the execution of transactions for the purchase,
sale, merger or takeover of companies. They negotiate and complete the deal on the
client's behalf, by working closely with lawyers and accountants. Mergers and acquisitions
analysts conduct operational and legal risk assessments of a company, assess comparable
companies in the market and help with the post-merger integration.
