## Description

Sectional belt mould assemblers operate the machine that presses belts into a V-shape. They stretch the belt around the mould and start the machine.

## Essential Skills

### Skills

- **create V-shape wrap** 
- **prepare assembled moulds for finishing** 
- **stretch belts** 
- **thread cloth liner** 
- **secure the liner**
