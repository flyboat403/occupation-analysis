## Description

Mattress making machine operators utilise machines to form mattresses. They create pads and coverings and cut, spread and attach the padding and cover material over the innerspring assemblies.

## Scope Note

Excludes people performing the manufacturing of inflatable rubber mattresses and rubber waterbed mattresses.
