Description
Metal production managers organise and manage the day-to-day and long-term project
work in a metal fabrication factory, to process basic metals into fabricated metals. They
create and schedule production plans, recruit new staff, enforce safety and company
policies, and strive for customer satisfaction through guaranteeing the product's quality.\
