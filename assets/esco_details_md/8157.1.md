Description
Laundry workers operate and monitor machines that use chemicals to wash or dry-clean
articles such as cloth and leather garments, linens, drapes or carpets, ensuring the color
and texture of these articles is being maintained. They work in laundry shops and
industrial laundry companies and sort the articles received from clients by fabric type.
They also determine the cleaning technique to be applied.
