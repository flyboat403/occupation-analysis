Description
Sailors assist the ship captain and any crew higher in hierarchy to operate ships. They dust
and wax furniture and polish wood trim, sweep floors and decks, and polish brass and
other metal parts. They inspect, repair, and maintain sails and rigging, and paint or varnish
surfaces. They make emergency repairs to the auxiliary engine. Sailors may stow supplies
and equipment and record data in log, such as weather conditions and distance travelled.
