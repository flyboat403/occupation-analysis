## Alternative Terms

- key and lock installer
- key and lock expert
- locksmith
- key maker
- key and lock specialist

## Description

Locksmiths install and repair mechanical and electronic lock systems using specialized tools. They cut and make duplicated keys for their clients and open locked doors in emergency situations. Locksmiths may also provide advice on security measures.
