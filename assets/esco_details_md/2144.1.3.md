Description
Automotive engineers design and oversee the manufacturing process and operation of
motor vehicles such as motorcycles, cars, trucks, buses and their respective engineering
systems. They design new vehicles or mechanical parts, supervise modifications and
resolve technical problems. They make sure the designs comply with cost specifications
and other constraints. They also conduct research studying environmental, energy and
safety aspects.
