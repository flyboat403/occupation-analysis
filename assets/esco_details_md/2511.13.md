Description
ICT system analysts specify the system needs to meet the end user requirements. They
analyse system functions in order to define their goals or purposes and to discover
operations and procedures for accomplishing them most efficiently. They also design new
IT solutions to improve business efficiency and productivity, produce outline designs and
estimate costs of new systems, specify the operations the system will perform, and the way
data will be viewed by the end user. They present the design to the users and work closely
with the users to implement the solution.
Scope note
Excludes people performing ICT programming.
