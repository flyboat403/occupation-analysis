Description
Coffee tasters taste coffee samples in order to evaluate the features of the product or to
prepare blending formulas. They determine the product's grade, estimate its market value,
and explore how these products may appeal to different consumer tastes. They write
blending formulas for workers who prepare coffee products for commercial purposes.
