Description
Energy managers coordinate the energy use in an organisation, and aim to implement
policies for increased sustainability, and minimisation of cost and environmental impact.
They monitor the energy demands and use, and develop improvement strategies, as well as
researching the most beneficial source of energy for the organisation's needs.
