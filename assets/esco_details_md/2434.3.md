Description
ICT presales engineers actively drive and manage the ICT evaluation stage of the sales
process, working in conjunction with the sales team. They provide technical guidance to
pre-sales personnel and plan and modify product ICT configurations to meet client
requirements. They pursue additional business development opportunities.
