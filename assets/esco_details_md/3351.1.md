Description
Customs officers combat the importation of illegal goods, firearms, drugs or other
dangerous or illegal items while checking the legality of items brought across national
borders. They are government officials who control the documents to ensure entry criteria
and custom laws are complied with and control if the custom taxes are paid correctly.
Scope note
Excludes customs and excise officer.
