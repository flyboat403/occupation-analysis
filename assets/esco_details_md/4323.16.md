Description
Ship planners manage the performance of a vessel. They ensure the safety of the vessel
and its cargo, its operationality and link available vessels to available cargos in order to
maximise the profitability of the voyages. They ensure that each container ship is loaded to
its optimal capacity, while keeping berth times and handling costs to a minimum. They also
plan the maintenance and overhaul of the ship, as well as the crew needed.
