Description
Contract managers oversee the respect of awarded contracts to ensure that services are
delivered as foreseen and that each step of the delivery is properly documented. They act
as the main point of contact for suppliers on contractual matters and report back to their
leadership. They implement financial and operational controls and ensure compliance with
all reporting requirements.
