## Alternative Terms

- futures trader
- arbitrageur
- futures contracts manager
- spreader

## Description

Futures traders undertake daily trading activities in the futures trading market by buying and selling futures contracts. They speculate on the futures contracts' direction, trying to make a profit by buying futures contracts they foresee to rise in price and sell contracts they foresee to fall in price.
