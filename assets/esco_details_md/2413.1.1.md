Description
Insurance rating analysts analyse information related to insurance markets and their credit
rating, prepare rating reports and invoices, compile financial data and present and explain
credit rating opinions to stakeholders, clients and external parties. They work for
insurance companies and calculate the insurance premium and rates for the company’s
clients using both manual and automated methods.
