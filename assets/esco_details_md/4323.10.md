Description
Pipeline route managers oversee the day-to-day operational aspects of transporting goods
via pipeline infrastructures. They have an overview of the network and plan the different
routes to transport goods from one site to another. They strive for the most efficient and
less costly route. They troubleshoot arising issues in networks and sites, ensure
implementation of regulations, and follow up the achievement of transport targets.
