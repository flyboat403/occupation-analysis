Description
Education welfare officers address the social and psychological well-being of students.
They counsel students concerning their personal issues that affect their school behaviour,
performance and social life. These issues may range from attention deficit problems, to
social and personal issues such as poverty or domestic and sexual abuse. Education
welfare officers also handle the communication between the students, the parents and the
school.
