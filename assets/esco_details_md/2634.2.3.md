Description
Health psychologists deal with the various aspects of health related behaviour of
individuals and groups, by helping individuals or groups prevent illness and promote
healthy behaviours by also providing counseling services. They perform tasks for the
development of health promotion activities and projects on the basis of psychological
science, research findings, theories, methods and techniques. They also engage in research
about health related issues to influence public policy on health care issues.
