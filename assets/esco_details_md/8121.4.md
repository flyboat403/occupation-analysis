Description
Metal drawing machine operators set up and operate drawing machines for ferrous and
non-ferrous metal products, designed to provide wires, bars, pipes, hollow profiles and
tubes with their specific form by reducing its cross-section and by pulling the working
materials through a series of drawing dies.
