Description
Correctional services managers manage the operations of a correctional facility. They
supervise personnel, develop and oversee correctional procedures, and ensure the
operation is compliant with legal regulations. They perform administrative duties and
facilitate cooperation with external institutions and staff providing aid to the facility.
