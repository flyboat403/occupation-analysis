Description
Harvest divers perform extraction and collection of marine resources, such as algae, coral,
razor shells, sea urchins and sponges, in a safe, competent and responsible manner to a
depth of 12 metres, using apnoea diving techniques as well as air supply equipment from
the surface, open-circuit.
