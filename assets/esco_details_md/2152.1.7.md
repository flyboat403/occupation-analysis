Code
2152.1.6
Description
Microelectronics engineers design, develop, and supervise the production of small
electronic devices and components such as micro-processors and integrated circuits.
Scope note
Excludes microsystem engineer. Excludes people using materials such as quartz, plastics
and ceramics.
