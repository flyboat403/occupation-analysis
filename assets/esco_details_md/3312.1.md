Description
Bank account managers advise prospective clients on the type of banking accounts suitable
for their needs. They work with clients to set up the bank account and remain their primary
point of contact in the bank, assisting with all necessary documentation. Bank account
managers may recommend their clients to contact other departments in the bank for other
specific needs.
