Description
Family planning counsellors provide support and counselling to adults and adolescents on
issues such as reproduction, contraceptive methods, pregnancy or termination of
pregnancy, in compliance with the legislation and practices. They also provide information
about maintaining optimum health practices, sexual disease prevention and treatment
recommendation referrals, working in collaboration with the professional doctors.
