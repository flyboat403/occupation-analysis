Description
Heads of workshop coordinate specialised workshops that construct, build, prepare, adapt
and maintain elements used on stage. Their work is based on artistic vision, schedules and
overall production documentation. They liaise with the designers involved in the
production, the production team and other services of the organisation.
Scope note
Includes people working in event and rental companies
