Description
Leather goods warehouse operators are in charge of the warehouse of leather,
components, other materials and production devises. They classify and register the
purchased raw materials and components, forecast purchases and distribute them across
different departments. They ensure that all the necessary raw materials and components
for the production are ready to be used and to be placed in the production chain.
