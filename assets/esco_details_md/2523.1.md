Description
ICT capacity planners ensure that the capacity of ICT services and ICT infrastructure is
able to deliver agreed service level targets in a cost effective and timely manner. They also
consider all resources required to deliver the appropriate ICT service, and plan for short,
medium, and long term business requirements.
