Description
Office managers oversee the administrative work that clerical workers are commissioned
to perform in various types of organisations or associations. They perform
micromanagement and maintain a close view of administrative processes such as such as
controlling correspondence, designing filing systems, reviewing and approving supply
requisitions, assigning and monitoring clerical functions. They report to managers within
the same department or to general managers in companies, depending on their size.
