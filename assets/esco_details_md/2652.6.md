Description
Disc jockeys mix music from various sources using turntables or a mixing console and play
music at events in front of a live audience. They may provide the music on the radio. They
select the music played on the radio and make sure that it is broadcasted according to
schedule. Disc jockeys can also create mixes for later distribution and playback.
