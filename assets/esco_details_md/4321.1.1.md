Description
Footwear factory warehouse operators are in charge of storing the raw and subsidiaries
materials, the working devices and the components for footwear production. They ensure
that all the components necessary for the production of shoes are ready to use in the
production chain by classifying and registering the purchased components, forecasting
purchases and distributing them across different departments.
