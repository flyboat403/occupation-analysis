Description
Rolling stock engineering technicians carry out technical functions to help rolling stock
engineers with the design, development, manufacturing and testing processes, installation
and maintenance of rail vehicles such as wagons, multiple units, carriages and locomotives.
They also conduct experiments, collect and analyse data and report their findings.
